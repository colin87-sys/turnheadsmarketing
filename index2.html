Straight answers

The questions every owner asks first.

Will it sound like a robot?

It sounds like a sharp, natural Australian receptionist — and it's upfront about being an assistant, because callers trust honesty more than a fake human. What callers actually care about is being answered fast, helped properly and booked in. That's what it does.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number and your provider. We simply divert the calls you'd otherwise miss — after hours, on busy overflow, or everything if you want full reception. Turn it off any time.

What does "usage at cost" actually mean?

Call minutes are billed at what they cost us — typically cents per minute, no markup, itemised on your invoice. For most businesses, a whole month of usage costs less than the margin on one recovered job.

What if a call is too complicated for it?

It does what a good receptionist does: takes the details, tells the caller exactly what happens next, and escalates — instant SMS to your team, or a live transfer to your mobile during set hours. Nothing gets dropped silently.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live within a week. Your total time investment is about 40 minutes: one build call, a quick diversion setup, and a launch check.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. After the 14-day trial it's month-to-month — cancel any time. We'd rather earn your business every month with leads than hold it hostage with paperwork.

What happens to my call data?

Recordings, transcripts and lead details belong to you, are stored securely, and are handled in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. We don't sell data, and we don't use your calls to help your competitors.
